Growth For Impact UI/UX Internship

What you'll be working on: growthforimpact.co, building LinkedIn for sustainability.

Role Overview:
We're seeking a creative and detail-oriented UX/UI Designer Intern to join Growth For Impact. As the first member of the design team, you will shape the user experience and visual design of our digital products, including our website and job board.

Key Responsibilities:
- Design and develop engaging user interfaces for the website and job board, with a seamless experience across devices and platforms.
- Conduct user research to understand user psychology and behavior, informing design decisions that meet user needs.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to develop and implement design solutions that align with business objectives.
- Create high-fidelity prototypes and design specifications using Figma.
- Participate in design critiques and feedback sessions to keep design quality and consistency high.
- Stay up to date with industry trends and best practices in UX/UI design.
- Maintain a cohesive design language and visual identity.

Expected Qualifications:
- Experience: 0 to 1 year of experience in UX/UI design. Recent graduates and people transitioning into design are encouraged to apply.
- Proficiency in UI/UX design principles and human-centered design methodologies.
- Experience with Figma and prototyping tools.
- Strong understanding of user psychology and behavior.
- Excellent aesthetic sense and attention to detail.

Mandatory Requirements:
- Must be great at understanding user psychology.
- Creative outlook, without fear of experimentation.
- An eye for detail.
- Great aesthetic sense.

Work environment: fully remote. The company values creativity, innovation, and sustainability.

First 90 days: help revamp the entire website, and build the best possible solution for the job board, incorporating user feedback and testing results.

Stipend: 10,000 to 15,000 per month.
